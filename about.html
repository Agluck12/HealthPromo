Our Story

A focused solution for a real industry need

HealthPromo was established in 2026 with a single purpose: to serve the promotional product needs of the healthcare industry better than anyone else.

Healthcare providers — from ABA therapy centers to large hospital systems — have always needed branded promotional items. But the traditional promo industry wasn't built with them in mind. Generic catalogs, confusing minimums, slow turnarounds, and reps who didn't understand the unique demands of healthcare settings left a real gap in the market.

We built HealthPromo to fill that gap — with a curated product selection, healthcare-specific expertise, and a streamlined process that gets your branded items in hand fast.

Industry-specific catalog Every product is chosen with healthcare and therapy environments in mind — from fidgets and stress relievers to apparel and drinkware.
Streamlined quoting No confusing spreadsheets or back-and-forth. Submit your request and we'll respond with pricing and a proof within one business day.
Backed by experience As a Bespoke Promotions subsidiary, we bring years of promotional product expertise to every order we fulfill.
