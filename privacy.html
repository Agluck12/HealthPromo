Privacy Policy

How HealthPromo collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: May 2026

Who We Are

HealthPromo ("we," "us," or "our") is a promotional products company based in Long Island, NY, operating as a subsidiary of Bespoke Promotions. We operate the website at healthpromo.net.

If you have any questions about this policy, you can reach us at info@healthpromo.net or by calling (516) 655-5423.

Information We Collect

We only collect information you voluntarily provide to us when submitting a quote request through our website. This may include:

  • Your first and last name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Organization or clinic name and type
  • Estimated order quantity
  • Products you're interested in
  • Any additional notes or questions you provide

We do not collect payment information, government IDs, or any sensitive personal data through this website.

How We Use Your Information

The information you submit is used solely to respond to your quote request. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Prepare and send you a pricing quote
  • Follow up regarding your order or inquiry
  • Provide digital proofs and product details relevant to your request

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Quote List (Browser Storage)

When you add products to your Quote List on our site, those items are stored locally in your browser's session storage. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to our servers until you submit a quote request. It is cleared automatically when you close your browser tab or complete a submission.

Third-Party Services

Our website uses the following third-party services to operate:

  • Google Apps Script — used to receive and log quote form submissions in a private Google Sheet accessible only to HealthPromo staff.
  • Netlify — our website is hosted on Netlify, which may log standard server access data (IP addresses, browser type, pages visited) for security and performance purposes. See Netlify's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts — we load fonts from Google's servers, which may log your IP address. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Cookies

We do not use tracking cookies or advertising cookies on this website. We do not use Google Analytics or any other behavioral tracking service. Your browsing activity on our site is not monitored or profiled.

Data Retention

Quote request information submitted through our forms is retained in our internal systems for as long as necessary to fulfill your request and maintain business records. If you would like your information removed, please contact us at info@healthpromo.net and we will delete it promptly.

Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for use by businesses and organizations, not individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
